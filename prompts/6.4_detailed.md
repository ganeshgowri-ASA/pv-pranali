# 6.4 Detailed Design — Schematic / SLD / P&ID / MCAD / Optical / BoM v1

## Purpose
Generate detailed design artefacts for P0 using Shilpasutra (MCAD) and
Vidyut (electrical) MCPs where available, otherwise produce structured
design stubs.

## Design Parameters (P0)

### Electrical — I-V Measurement Board
- 4-wire Kelvin connection to DUT (module under test)
- Current range: 0–20 A (2-module parallel)
- Voltage range: 0–100 V (2-module series)
- Resolution: 10 mV / 1 mA
- ADC: 24-bit differential
- Scan speed: full I-V curve in <200 ms (steady-state)
- Interface: USB/LAN to host PC
- Isolation: 1500 V DC per IEC 61010-1

### Power Supply (MH variant)
- Input: 3-phase 415 V AC, 50 Hz
- Ballast: electronic, dimmable 50–100%
- Lamp: MH 6 kW or 12 kW (per area requirement)
- Ignitor: integrated electronic

### Power Supply (LED variant)
- Input: 3-phase 415 V AC, 50 Hz
- LED driver: CC, PWM-dimmable, closed-loop irradiance feedback
- LED array: multi-junction, spectral bands matched to AM1.5G

### Temperature Control
- Platen heater: 1 kW ceramic IR or resistive
- Cooling: Peltier or mechanical refrigeration
- Sensor: Pt-100 (4-wire), IEC 60751 Class AA
- Control: PID, ±0.5 °C setpoint stability

### Optical
- Integrating tunnel / light guide: diffuse reflective coating
- Spatial uniformity target: ±2% over 2-module test area (Class A+)
- Measurement plane: 2000 × 1100 mm (2× full-size module)

## BoM v1 Schema
```csv
item,description,manufacturer,mpn,quantity,unit,estimated_unit_cost_inr,notes
```

## Steps
1. Call Vidyut MCP (`dry_run=True`) to generate schematic stubs.
2. Call Shilpasutra MCP (`dry_run=True`) to generate MCAD stubs.
3. Populate BoM v1 with major items (lamp/LED array, ballast/driver,
   I-V board, platen, sensors, enclosure, cabling).
4. Flag long-lead items (>12 weeks) in BoM notes column.
5. Output all artefacts to paths above.

## Constraints
- `dry_run=True` for all MCP calls.
- STEP files are stubs; full MCAD detail in Phase 2.
