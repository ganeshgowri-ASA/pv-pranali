## MH-vs-LED Trade Study

### Criteria
| Criterion | Weight | MH Score | LED Score | Notes |
|-----------|--------|----------|-----------|-------|
| Capital cost | 20% | 4 | 2 | MH ~50% cheaper |
| Spectral match (IEC 60904-9 Class A+) | 25% | 3 | 5 | LED tunable |
| Temporal non-uniformity | 15% | 3 | 5 | LED steady-state superior |
| Spatial uniformity (2-module) | 20% | 3 | 4 | LED array scalable |
| Lamp life / maintenance | 10% | 2 | 5 | LED >50kh vs MH 1–2kh |
| Irradiance stability (700–1300 W/m²) | 10% | 3 | 5 | LED closed-loop |
| RIL URS compliance | — | Pass | Pass | Both achievable |

**Weighted score**: MH = 3.1 | LED = 4.2

### Recommendation
LED variant preferred for long-term TCO and spectral tunability. MH variant
offered as budget option (USD 100k) with LED as standard (USD 220k).
